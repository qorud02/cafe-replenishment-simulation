# Journal manuscript, 12 October 2026

**Pack sizes and expiry information in shared ingredient cafe replenishment** — Kyunghan Bae.

This 7-page Technical Note examines three replenishment rules using simulated demand, 54 factorial settings and 200 evaluation replications per setting. It includes a complete 54-row table of paired primary comparisons, long-horizon checks and calibration-boundary sensitivity.

- [Manuscript PDF](Cafe_replenishment_Technical_Note.pdf)
- [Editable LaTeX source and figures](source.zip); build instructions are included.
- [Computational archive and earlier manuscript](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23298824)

The median relative cost change after separate calibration is -0.41%, and the largest cost decrease is 7.54%. In the largest-decrease setting, expired milk increases by 0.91 liters per day. The experiments describe generated demand and inventory, with no actual shop observations.
